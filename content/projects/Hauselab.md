#### [Hauselab](https://www.hauselab.com)

## Overview

I built Hauselab as a fitness discovery place. The goal is to help myself and people around me to explore training disciplines, studios, and routines without feeling intimidated. Hauselab focuses on clarity and approachability. It frames fitness as something to understand first, not commit to immediately.

## Key Features

- Curated discovery of fitness disciplines such as Hyrox, running, gym, and yoga.
    
- Content designed to reduce anxiety for beginners.
    
- Clear explanations of what to expect before joining a class or studio.
    
- Editorial visual direction that makes fitness feel accessible.
    
- Emphasis on exploration rather than sign ups or transactions.
    

## What I Learned

- Discovery problems are emotional, not functional.
    
- Fear blocks action more than lack of information.
    
- Curation builds confidence faster than exhaustive listings.
    
- Visual tone determines who feels welcome.
    

## Why I Built This

I saw how fitness discovery is fragmented and biased toward extremes. Many people want to train but hesitate because they feel out of place before they start. I built Hauselab to remove that friction and help people feel informed and confident before making decisions.
