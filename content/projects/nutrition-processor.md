## Overview

A weekend project exploring AI-assisted meal planning. Takes user dietary preferences and health goals to generate personalized weekly nutrition plans with macro tracking.

## Technical Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js 14
- **AI:** OpenAI GPT-4
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
- **Deployment:** Vercel

## Key Features

- AI-powered meal suggestions
- Macro nutrient tracking
- Dietary restriction support (vegan, keto, etc.)
- Weekly meal planning
- Shopping list generation

## What I Learned

This project taught me about prompt engineering, streaming responses, and how to build AI-powered product features. I also learned about rate limiting and cost management when working with AI APIs.

## Why I Built This

As a PM, understanding AI capabilities firsthand helps me better evaluate AI feature proposals and set realistic expectations for engineering timelines. This hands-on experience has been invaluable in product discussions.
